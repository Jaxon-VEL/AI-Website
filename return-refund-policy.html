Return & Refund Policy

Clear guidance for order changes, damaged shipments, and refund requests.

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

We are dedicated to offering high-quality products and excellent customer support. Because of the specialized nature of research-grade materials, this policy differs from standard retail stores.

Research Use Only & Return Policy

Due to strict safety, quality-assurance, and product-integrity protocols, we cannot accept returns on products once they have left our facility.

Items that have been opened, used, or exposed to improper storage conditions are not eligible for return, exchange, or store credit.

Damaged, Missing, or Incorrect Items

If your package arrives damaged, is missing an item, or contains the wrong product, notify us within 48 hours of delivery.

To help us resolve the issue quickly, please email:

  • Your order number.
  • A brief description of what went wrong.
  • Clear photos of the item(s), shipping box, and shipping label.

Once we review the request, we will resolve it with either a replacement or a refund as appropriate.

How Refunds Work

Refunds are handled case by case and are typically granted if:

  • You canceled your order before it was shipped.
  • An incorrect item was sent and cannot be replaced.
  • Your package was severely damaged during transit and verified through photos or a carrier claim.
  • An error occurred directly on our end.

Processing Time: Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method. Allow 5 to 10 business days for your financial institution to process and post the credit.

Order Cancellations & Changes

We pack and ship quickly. If you need to change or cancel an order, contact us immediately. Once an order enters processing or has been dispatched, it can no longer be modified or canceled.

Shipping Issues & Lost Packages

If tracking shows a delay, loss, or indicates delivery but you cannot find the package, check with the carrier first. If you still need help afterward, reach out to our team and we will help work toward a resolution.

Questions or Issues? Reach Out First.

If you experience a problem with a purchase, contact our support team before filing a credit-card dispute or chargeback. We are here to resolve genuine concerns fairly and quickly.
