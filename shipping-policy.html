Shipping Policy

Fast, safe delivery information for Valor Elite Labs orders.

Shipping & Delivery Information

Fast, safe delivery is our priority. We carefully pack every order to help it arrive in optimal condition.

How We Deliver

Free 2–3 Day Delivery

All standard shipments are sent via USPS or UPS and typically arrive within 2 to 3 business days across the continental United States.

Quick Handling

Orders ship within 1 business day of purchase, excluding weekends and official holidays.

Real-Time Tracking

As soon as your order leaves our facility, we will email a tracking code so you can follow delivery progress.

Temperature-Controlled & Protective Packaging

Your shipment is packed with care using:

  • Impact-resistant cushioning.
  • Sealed, moisture-proof containers.
  • Cold packs or temperature-controlled insulation when necessary.

Damaged Shipments & Insurance

Every package includes shipping insurance. If an order arrives damaged, contact support with photos of the damage and we will review the issue for a replacement or other appropriate resolution.

Shipping Territory

We currently ship within the United States only.
