Privacy Policy

How Valor Elite Labs collects, uses, and safeguards information.

Information Collection Policy

This policy describes our information practices and will be updated as our services and legal obligations evolve.

Information Collection

1. Usage & Analytics Data (Non-Identifiable)

Details: IP address, browser type, referral URLs, timestamps, and site interactions.

Purpose: To monitor platform stability, analyze site performance, and improve navigation. This data is kept strictly non-personal.

2. Account & Transaction Data (Personal)

Details: Name, contact information, billing/shipping addresses, and order records.

Purpose: Collected only when you create an account, place an order, or send an inquiry to support.

Cookies & Information Usage

Cookies Policy

Function: Stores preferences and enhances browser interaction.

Control: Can be managed or disabled via browser settings; this may impact site functionality.

Use of Information

Personal and usage data is utilized to:

  • Complete checkout and process payments.
  • Manage order logistics, fulfillment, and status notifications.
  • Provide customer support and resolve inquiries.
  • Optimize overall platform performance and user experience.

Data Integrity

We never sell, rent, or trade personal customer data to external marketers or third parties.

Communications & Security Policy

Email Notifications

Transactional: Order confirmations, shipping updates, and account notices.

Promotional: Optional updates and offers; unsubscribe anytime via the link in the footer.

SMS Messaging

Usage: Service alerts and order status updates, if a mobile number is provided.

Opt-Out: Follow reply instructions in text messages or contact customer support directly.

Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply based on your carrier.

Data & Payment Security

Protection: Standard encryption and security protocols safeguard your data.

Payment Handling: All payment details are processed through encrypted, secure gateways. Full credit card numbers are never stored on our servers.

Third-Party Integrations, Disclaimers & Updates

Third-Party Service Providers

Authorized Uses: Essential operations only, such as payment gateways and freight/shipping carriers.

Restrictions: Partners are restricted from using customer data for marketing or non-operational purposes.

Research Use Only (RUO) Notice

Scope: Products are restricted to laboratory research and development only.

Prohibition: Strictly not for human, clinical, therapeutic, or diagnostic use.

Regulatory Designation: Not an FDA-evaluated medical device/drug; not a 503A or 503B compounding/outsourcing facility under the FD&C Act.
