Valor Elite Labs respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the steps we take to safeguard it.

Like many websites, Valor Elite Labs collects non-personal usage data to understand how visitors use the site. This information is used for analytics and site improvement and is not linked to personally identifiable data.

2. Personal Information

Personal information may be collected when you place an order, create an account, or contact support. This may include your name, email address, billing and shipping address, and order details.

We use the information we collect to:

Data Integrity

Email & SMS Communications

We may send emails for order confirmations, shipping updates, account notices, or support responses. Optional promotional emails may also be sent based on your preferences. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails or by contacting us.

If you provide a mobile number, it may be used for service-related messages, including order updates and account alerts. Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out by following the instructions in the message or by emailing us.

Data Security

We use standard security measures to protect your information. Sensitive payment details are handled through secure, encrypted payment processors. Valor Elite Labs does not store full credit card information on its servers.
